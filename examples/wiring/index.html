Requirements and Capabilities

Everything on this page is computed from manifests alone — nothing is loaded until you press Load the resolvable ones. That is what resolution is: it answers whether a module could run, before a single line of it is fetched.

Offered

Derived from each manifest, plus whatever it declares itself. Identity and service capabilities are not written by hand — they follow from provides and from having an id at all.

Wired

One requirement, one capability, one module — or a reason why not.

Waiting, and waiting in vain

The distinction this whole model buys. A module whose service nobody promises can never run, and that is knowable now; one whose provider merely has not started yet is a different case entirely.
